High-Altitude Safety Protocol (Uttarakhand State)
Always register your ascent with the local Forest Range Post in Uttarkashi, Chamoli, or Rudraprayag. Never trek alone on glaciated trails above 4,000 meters, and adhere strictly to the golden rule of acclimatization: climb high, sleep low.

Safety & Environment
