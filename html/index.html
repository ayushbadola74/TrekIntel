In the Himalayas, weather is the single most critical factor for expedition success. Our Open-Meteo integration retrieves dynamic high-altitude forecasts before you lace up your boots.

Sub-Zero Apparent Temperature

Understand the exact 'feels like' chill factor generated by high-altitude winds, so you can pack suitable thermal layers.
